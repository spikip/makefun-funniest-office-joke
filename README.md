## About this entry

The brief rewards a real joke with a setup and a turn, built on something
anyone who has worked in an office recognises, with the punchline last.
Trust falls are the perfect setup: a team building exercise whose entire
point is that your coworkers will catch you. The turn is that fourteen of
them do the opposite in perfect unison, stepping aside in agile
formation - a phrase that makes a corporate ritual out of the betrayal.
The narrator falls backwards into nothing while the team performs its
choreography, and the joke lands because the reader has sat through
exactly this kind of exercise. One line, a hundred and thirty-three
characters, punchline last. The program holds the line, checks it against
the two hundred character cap, and writes it to /output/entry.txt.

## Run it

```sh
python3 main.py
```

This writes the one-line office joke to `/output/entry.txt`.
